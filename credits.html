Earth imagery

The Earth image on the Sercle home page and Account page is adapted from “Eastern Hemisphere – Blue Marble 2012”, a composite of Suomi NPP VIIRS satellite data compiled by Norman Kuring, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. Credit: NASA/NOAA. Source: NASA Image and Video Library (ID GSFC_20171208_Archive_e001788).

Sercle modified the image: cropped and resized it, adjusted the lighting, and added a circular mask and an atmosphere glow.

AI-generated imagery

The World artwork on this site was created by Sercle with AI image generation (OpenAI ChatGPT). Two images (Lens, Stage) were locally retouched to remove artefacts. Some background imagery was generated procedurally, and icons were hand-coded, with AI assistance (Anthropic Claude).
